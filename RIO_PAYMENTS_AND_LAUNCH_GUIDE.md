# Rio's South African Payments & Merchant Setup Guide

> **Purpose:** This guide walks you through how Antigravity builds the payment code vs. what you need to do with South African payment providers (**Paystack**, **Peach Payments / PayGate**, and **PayJustNow**) to get your live API keys and go live safely.

---

## 1. How the Workflow Works (Code First, Keys Second)
You do **not** need live merchant accounts before running `Chat 5`.

1. **Step 1 (`Chat 5` builds the integration):** Antigravity writes all the Flutter checkout screens, backend webhook handlers, signature verification, and idempotency checks using **Sandbox (Test) Keys**. You can test fake payments, 75/25 cancellations, and wallet deductions right away.
2. **Step 2 (Deploy the Public Website):** `Chat 5` also builds `public-site/`. Deploy that website to Cloudflare Pages first, because South African payment providers inspect your live website before approving your merchant account.
3. **Step 3 (Apply for Merchant Accounts & Get API Keys):** You submit your FICA/business documents to the providers below.
4. **Step 4 (Plug in the Keys):** Once approved, you paste your **Public Key**, **Secret Key**, and **Webhook Secret** into your Firebase Functions environment config (never hard-coded in Dart or pushed to GitHub) and switch from Sandbox to Live.

---

## 2. Which South African Providers to Use

### A. Primary Card, Apple Pay & Instant EFT Gateway (Pick One)
- **Option 1: Paystack South Africa (Fastest to Start)**
  - **Pros:** Very quick online onboarding (supports sole proprietors and PTY Ltds), great developer API, built-in **Card Tokenisation** (great for saving cards and future R1 promos), Instant EFT, Apple Pay, and clean webhooks.
  - **Website:** `paystack.com/za`
- **Option 2: Peach Payments / PayGate (Enterprise SA Standard)**
  - **Pros:** Supports almost every South African bank and payment method (Card, Ozow/Instant EFT, SnapScan, Apple Pay) and integrates smoothly with split/marketplace flows.
  - **Cons:** Stricter underwriting; usually takes 5 to 10 working days for full FICA approval.
  - **Website:** `peachpayments.com`

*(Tip: Starting with **Paystack** for fast launch + **PayJustNow** for Buy-Now-Pay-Later is the quickest path to taking real money in Week 1, and you can also enable Peach/PayGate anytime through the same payment adapter built in `Chat 5`.)*

### B. Buy Now, Pay Later (BNPL): PayJustNow
- **How it works:** The client pays over 3 interest-free monthly instalments, while **PayJustNow settles the full amount to you upfront** (minus their merchant fee).
- **Important note on approval:** PayJustNow requires you to contact their merchant onboarding team (`payjustnow.com/merchants`) and usually expects a registered SA business (PTY Ltd), a business bank account, and a live website with clear refund/cancellation terms.
- **How we handle it in `Chat 5`:** We build the PayJustNow checkout button behind a **Feature Flag** (`enablePayJustNowBnpl`). That way, if Paystack approves you in 48 hours while PayJustNow takes 2 weeks to approve your account, you can launch immediately with Card + Instant EFT and flip PayJustNow ON in your Admin Dashboard the moment their keys arrive.

## 3. Documents Checklist (Have These Ready Before Applying)
Every regulated South African payment gateway will ask for FICA verification:
1. **Your South African ID** (Smart ID card or green barcoded ID).
2. **Proof of Residential Address** (utility bill or bank statement under 3 months old).
3. **Business Registration (CIPC):** Your PTY Ltd registration certificate (COR14.3) if registered, or Sole Proprietor details (Paystack accepts sole proprietors; Peach and PayJustNow generally require a PTY Ltd).
4. **Business Bank Account Confirmation Letter** (stamped or downloaded PDF from FNB, Capitec Business, Standard Bank, Nedbank, or TymeBank).
5. **Live Public Website URL** containing:
   - Clear description of services (verified nail appointments in CPT and JHB),
   - **Cancellation & Refund Policy** (explaining the 75%/25% late-cancellation wallet rule and 100% tech-cancellation credit),
   - **POPIA Privacy Policy** and **Terms & Conditions**,
   - Support email address (e.g., `support@yourdomain.co.za`) and city presence.

## 4. How to Plug the Keys Into the App Safely (Never Push Keys to GitHub)

Once a provider approves your account, their dashboard gives you two sets of keys: **Test/Sandbox** and **Live**.

1. **Test Keys (For Final QA):**
   - Put Test Public Key in `abe_app/.env` (or `--dart-define`).
   - Put Test Secret Key and Webhook Secret into Firebase Functions config:
     ```powershell
     firebase functions:secrets:set PAYMENT_SECRET_KEY
     firebase functions:secrets:set PAYMENT_WEBHOOK_SECRET
     firebase functions:secrets:set PAYJUSTNOW_API_KEY
     ```
   - Copy the **Webhook URL** generated by `firebase deploy --only functions` and paste it into your Paystack / Peach / PayJustNow dashboard webhook settings.
2. **Run 3 Live R5 Test Bookings:**
   - Switch to **Live Keys**, book a R5 test item using your own bank card, verify the booking moves to `confirmed`, verify the 4-digit PIN appears on the client screen, and check the `financial_ledger` entry in your Admin Dashboard.
3. **Never Commit Secrets:**
   - Ensure `.env`, `*.key`, and `google-services.json` / service account JSON keys stay in `.gitignore`.
