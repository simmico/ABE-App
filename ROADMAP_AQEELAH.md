# Aqeelah's Beauty Empire (ABE): Master Roadmap (Aqeelah's Edition)

> **How this roadmap works:** Instead of strict daily deadlines that create unnecessary pressure, our journey is organised into **18 focused items across 5 stages**. You only ever need to worry about your **Current Focus** item. When you finish it and tick it off in the Founder Hub (`founder-hub/index.html`), your progress bar moves up automatically and your next item appears. Your tasks are highlighted in the main callouts below, with Rio's tasks right underneath so you and Rio can both see what the other is working on.

---

## Stage 1: Getting Comfortable and Understanding the Market
**Our Goal:** Learn to look at the Cape Town (CPT) and Johannesburg (JHB) nail markets with founder eyes, define what quality means for our brand, and set up the core app workspace.

### Item 1: Defining Our Quality Standard
> [!IMPORTANT]
> **AQEELAH'S FOCUS (CPT):**
> - [ ] **Set up the Master Research Sheet:** Create a Google Sheet with two tabs: *Nail Techs* and *Independent Salons*. Add columns for: Name, City (CPT/JHB), Instagram/TikTok Handle, Base Gel/Acrylic Price, Booking Method, and Notes.
> - [ ] **Write the "Certified Pro" Quality Checklist:** Write down the 5 to 7 non-negotiable things that separate an amazing nail set from a bad one (cuticle neatness, apex structure, shaping consistency, hygiene, retention).
> - *Growth Connection:* **Self-Belief and Authority.** Writing the rules reminds you that you already know the beauty industry from the inside. You are the expert who decides whose work is good enough for our platform.
