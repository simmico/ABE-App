Cancellation, Refund & POPIA Policies

Clear rules protecting clients, nail technicians, and partner salons across South Africa.

Cancellation & Wallet Refunds

Client late cancellations credit 75% to the client's closed-loop ABE Wallet and allocate 25% to the nail tech's earnings ledger. If a technician cancels, the client receives 100% ABE Wallet credit plus a single-use apology voucher. Client ABE Wallet balances are closed-loop and are applied automatically at checkout (non-withdrawable).

POPIA Privacy Compliance

Personal information is collected strictly for booking verification, commercial venue safety checks, and payment settlement in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). Card details are tokenised by licensed South African gateways; raw card numbers are never stored by ABE.
